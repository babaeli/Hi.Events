# 🎯 KEY DIFFERENCES SUMMARY

## 🎪 **Organizer Focus:**
- Manage THEIR events
- Sell tickets
- Track THEIR revenue
- Communicate with THEIR attendees
- Build THEIR brand

## 👑 **Super Admin Focus:**
- Oversee ENTIRE platform
- Monitor ALL organizers
- Track TOTAL revenue (including platform fees)
- Provide customer support
- Ensure platform health
- Manage platform growth

# 💡 **Use Case Examples**

## Organizer Scenario:
```
Music Festival Promoter logs in:
├── Creates "Summer Music Fest 2026"
├── Sets up 5 ticket types ($50-$500)
├── Adds promo codes for early birds
├── Tracks sales in real-time
├── Sends email to 1,000 ticket buyers
└── Makes $50,000 in sales
    └── Keeps $45,000 (after platform fee)
```

## Super Admin Scenario:
```
Platform Owner logs in:
├── Sees 50 active organizers
├── Views 200 total events this month
├── Tracks $2M in platform-wide sales
├── Collects $200K in platform fees
├── Notices organizer #23 needs help
├── Impersonates them
├── Fixes their ticket configuration
└── Stops impersonation
```
