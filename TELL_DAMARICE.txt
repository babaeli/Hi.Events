Hi Damarice! 👋

I found and fixed the problem where usernames and links don't work when
you deploy the app publicly!

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                         THE PROBLEM
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

When deployed to Render/Vercel/Koyeb, these were MISSING:
  ❌ VITE_FRONTEND_URL
  ❌ APP_URL
  ❌ APP_FRONTEND_URL
  ❌ APP_CDN_URL

Without them:
  - Links showed "http://localhost" instead of your real domain
  - Organizer profile URLs were broken
  - Event URLs didn't work
  - Images failed to load
  - Email links were wrong

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                         THE FIX
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

I've updated these files with the missing variables:
  ✅ render.yaml
  ✅ vercel.json
  ✅ koyeb.yml
  ✅ frontend/.env.production

All changes are pushed to GitHub:
  📍 Repository: babaeli/Hi.Events
  📍 Branch: feature/my-contribution
  📍 Latest commit: 5c389204

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                    WHAT YOU NEED TO DO
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

OPTION 1 (PULL FROM GITHUB):
  1. Go to: https://github.com/Damarice/Hi.Events
  2. Pull latest from: babaeli/Hi.Events (feature/my-contribution)
  3. Render will auto-redeploy
  4. Wait 3-5 minutes
  5. ✅ Test your URLs!

OPTION 2 (MANUAL - FASTER):
  1. Go to: https://dashboard.render.com
  2. Click your "hi-events" service
  3. Go to "Environment" tab
  4. Add these variables:

     VITE_FRONTEND_URL = https://hi-events-g3dx.onrender.com
     APP_URL = https://hi-events-g3dx.onrender.com
     APP_FRONTEND_URL = https://hi-events-g3dx.onrender.com
     APP_CDN_URL = https://hi-events-g3dx.onrender.com/storage

  5. Click "Save Changes"
  6. Click "Manual Deploy" → "Clear build cache & deploy"
  7. Wait 3-5 minutes
  8. ✅ Test your URLs!
