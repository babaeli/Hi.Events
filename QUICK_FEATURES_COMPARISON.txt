════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
📈 REVENUE MODEL
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

When Organizer sells a $100 ticket:

    $100.00 (Ticket Price)
    ├─ $10.00 (10% Platform Fee)           → Goes to SUPER ADMIN
    ├─ $0.50  (Fixed Platform Fee)         → Goes to SUPER ADMIN
    └─ $89.50 (Organizer Revenue)          → Goes to ORGANIZER

Super Admin earns: $10.50 per ticket
Organizer keeps: $89.50 per ticket
